## 00:00–00:20 · The problem and product

Bring your community together around a cause.
Together helps schools, clubs, and nonprofits create a branded fundraising website, launch campaigns, and offer engaging ways to take part.
Greenwood School is raising funds for classroom supplies that benefit every pupil.
Product prototype. Payments and selected services are simulated.

## 00:20–00:50 · Your organization, your website

Start with your organization’s name, public address, and the features it needs.
Choose campaign review, sharing tools, partner rewards, or sponsor-funded play.
Add your logo, colours, message, and imagery, then preview the branded experience on desktop and mobile.
Publish your organization’s fundraising website, ready for its first campaign.

## 00:50–01:30 · Create and publish a campaign

An organizer uses a guided assistant or the full form; both work on the same saved draft.
They provide the purpose, beneficiary, {$60,000|sixty thousand dollars} target, dates, participation choices, and next supporter-update commitment.
Memory Match sits alongside direct giving, and an eligible partner offer can be attached.
The assistant is a simulated, rules-based guide, not a live {AI|A I} service.
When review is required, an organization reviewer approves the campaign.
The approved version becomes public only after the separate publish action.

## 01:30–01:55 · Share it with the community

One published campaign link powers the sharing kit.
The same address appears in its generated QR code, poster, message drafts, and social-image caption.
Share one campaign through the channels your community already uses.
Prototype data remains in this browser and is not synchronized to another device.

## 01:55–02:35 · The supporter experience

Supporters can read the purpose, beneficiary, target, progress, updates, and planned use of funds before participating.
They can give an amount directly or choose Play to support.
In Memory Match, each completed pair increases a suggestion without interrupting play.
No payment happens while playing.
The supporter reviews and edits the final amount, then confirms a simulated contribution.
Only that confirmation changes the campaign total.
The thank-you page shows the amount and a receipt saved on this device.

## 02:35–03:00 · Sponsor-funded participation

Sponsor-funded play is available when an organization records an eligible fictional commitment.
Northwind Stationery supports this Memory Match campaign.
A completed pair unlocks a pending reward without adding to the player’s checkout.
It counts toward campaign funds only after an admin confirms the simulated sponsor funds.
Other games are not shown as sponsor eligible.

## 03:00–03:25 · Local partner rewards

Partners can offer eligible supporters a reward with clear conditions and an expiry.
After a qualifying contribution, Mia claims Corner Stationery’s ten-percent offer.
The voucher shows the merchant, QR code, backup code, and terms.
Assigned store staff record a simulated redemption; no till or payment service is connected.

## 03:25–03:40 · Personal sharing

Supporters can share the cause in their own words through a personalized page and referral link.
Contributions through Mia’s link still support the original Greenwood campaign.
Attribution is counted once, not as a second total.

## 03:40–04:15 · Transparency after giving

Later in the campaign, supporters can keep following what the organizer reports.
The timeline shows the next update, published news, and delivery milestones.
Fund use separates planned spending, confirmed simulated contributions, and organizer-reported expenses.
Evidence is labelled attached or not uploaded, never independently verified.
After the {$60,000|sixty thousand dollars} target is reached, fundraising can close while delivery remains in progress.
Reaching the target is one milestone, not the end of the work.
